# PT Website Pattern Research

Reviewed October 14, 2026. Method: search for physical therapy clinic and movement/rehabilitation websites, then inspect the public pages below. Six comparison clinics, from small independents to networks, plus the target. This is a qualitative convenience sample, not market-wide measurement. Observations about content are based on live page retrieval; Mendocino homepage composition was also inspected in a browser screenshot. We cannot infer the designer's effort or template provenance from appearance.

| Clinic / Primary Source | Observed Pattern | Creative Implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Mendocino Physical Therapy](https://www.mendocinopt.com/) | Outdoor hero, movement slogan, broad benefit claims, expertise list; fitness and golf on additional pages | Outdoor imagery alone is already present. Differentiate through interaction, typography and specific local-life goals. |
| [Movement Physical Therapy, Chicago](https://www.movementphysicaltherapy.com/) | Return-to-activities promise, one-on-one care, technique grid, team, locations, testimonials | Personalized care and return-to-life language are category conventions, not a unique positioning claim. |
| [Shore Movement](https://www.shoremovement.com/) | Moving-again headline, treatment inventory, individualized care, clinician biography, hours and contact | Replace brochure order with a visitor goal while retaining practical clarity. |
| [Motion](https://motionclinics.org/) | Evidence/inclusion positioning, people and team, locations, scheduling | Already a thoughtful counterexample to generic clinical copy. Human language alone will not distinguish us. |
| [Athletico](https://www.athletico.com/) | Care/service navigation, locations, appointment acquisition and resource content | Keep contact direct; avoid replicating network-scale navigation at a single clinic. |
| [Select Physical Therapy](https://www.selectphysicaltherapy.com/) | Return-to-life promise, body-area navigation, services, trust and appointment routes | A body map is already conventional. A local-life goal selector offers a different entry. |
| [Rehab United](https://www.rehabunited.com/) | Functional approach, personalized plans, causes/impairments explanation, provider and location navigation | Movement/functional care is substantive but not exclusive brand territory. |

## What Repeats In This Sample
1. Broad recovery/movement language with interchangeable benefit claims.
2. Services, conditions or body areas form the main information architecture.
3. Expertise is presented through techniques, clinicians and credentials.
4. Appointment/contact and locations recur as conversion mechanics.
5. People, activity and treatment imagery support the care promise.

Not every site uses every pattern. No sampled evidence supports claiming all PT websites look identical, or that all are blue/white, stock-photo templates. The new direction should invert familiar storytelling, not remove trust, accessibility or contact basics.

## Mendocino Findings
Homepage browser screenshot showed serif heading on outdoor photography and a compact mobile menu. The visible 55+ class Learn More link returned to the homepage, adding little guidance. Site uses Squarespace-hosted image URLs; this does not establish which template was used.

Publicly advertised differentiators: one-on-one PT, fitness-forward care, 55+ Legends partnership with Crossfit Firefly, clinic fitness/mobility classes, and TPI golf assessments. Source routes and high-risk copy disposition are in ROUTES-AND-FACTS.md. These form a real basis for a distinctive concept, without invented equipment or patient success stories.

## Recommendation
Build a compact editorial movement atlas: everyday goals become the entry points, one original contour motif ties the experience together, and concrete practice facts substantiate the emotional direction. Judge differentiation by whether the concept could plausibly belong to this Ukiah practice and whether visitors can still find care/contact immediately.
