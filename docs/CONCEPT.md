# Life, In Motion

## Creative Thesis
The visitor arrives thinking about something they miss doing. Let that human goal organize the first interaction. Use Mendocino's actual combination of rehabilitation, everyday fitness, older-adult classes and golf to make a website that feels like a local movement journal.

Proposed headline: **More Life. In Every Move.**
Opening prompt: **What Do You Want Back?**
These are creative proposals, not clinical promises or approved practice branding.

## The Signature Moment
An original continuous line travels through a restrained illustrated landscape. Four large keyboard-operable choices change the activity caption, care explanation and the line's destination: The Outdoors, Everyday Strength, Life After Surgery, A Full Round. The same visual grammar becomes terrain contour, walking path, lifting arc and golf swing. The local demonstrator uses abstract SVG figures and paths to prove the interaction without unlicensed photos. The illustrations are visibly identified as conceptual.

For a later production treatment, commission documentary photos of real local movement and consenting staff. Let a single illustrated line bridge photo and diagram. Do not fabricate patients, clinic interiors or before-and-after results. Full animation is optional; the static state must communicate everything.

## Visual Direction
Warm paper, near-black redwood green, clay and ochre; a bold editorial serif with practical sans-serif controls; oversized but content-sized headings; small numbered captions; thin contour rules. Compact rows replace promotional card grids. A pale technical-paper panel supports the dark scene, producing a distinct editorial composition without empty viewport panels or scroll hijacking.

## Homepage Sequence
1. Masthead: practice name, Care, Visit, phone action. Clear independent-concept label.
2. Goal-led opening: headline, four choices, changing movement illustration and short source-grounded care note.
3. Three compact practice pathways: one-on-one PT, fitness/mobility, golf. Each opens useful detail.
4. Visit: source-listed address, hours and office number; coverage questions lead to calling, not assumptions about benefits.
5. Existing copyright and integrated lower-right ComputerWorks.ai credit.

## Invert The Familiar Pattern
| Common Pattern | Proposed Alternative | Preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad recovery slogan | Specific everyday ambition plus changeable scene | Hopeful, plain language |
| Treatment grid first | Goal first, actual service detail immediately after | Discoverable care information |
| Generic outdoor hero | Original movement atlas and later commissioned local documentary work | Sense of place |
| Technical expertise wall | Show how care relates to a goal; details in disclosures | Accurate credentials and clinical boundaries |
| Repeated appointment banners | Persistent compact call action and one visit area | An obvious next step |

## Production Extension
Proposed routes: Home, Physical Therapy, Fitness & Mobility, Golf Performance, Your First Visit. Preserve source routes with deliberate redirects if a migration is authorized. Add people/therapist content only after names, credentials and consent are confirmed. No guessed booking integration: an existing source class-booking host is evidence for review, not authority to connect it.

## Accessibility And Trust
Real buttons, visible focus, live announcement of changed copy, large touch targets, text equivalents for visual goals, no movement required, no diagnostic quiz or prescription. The selector changes editorial content only and stores/transmits nothing. Clinical review remains mandatory for production. Include older adults, ordinary chores and modest goals in final photography, not only high-performance athletes.

## Commercial Rationale And Review
Recommendation: a distinctive visual system could make the prospect pitch more memorable and communicate the breadth of the practice. No conversion lift, booking volume or revenue increase is established. Initial review: can the owner recognize the practice, select a relevant goal and find a phone/location quickly? If later approved, evaluate task completion with consenting representative users before choosing any privacy-reviewed analytics.

## Scope Of This Delivery
Research, Linear structure, local scaffold and an interactive art-direction demonstrator. The full site, commissioned media, practice review, online scheduling, publication and production migration are future gates. Exactly one next action: owner reviews the local concept and records accept, revise or hold.
