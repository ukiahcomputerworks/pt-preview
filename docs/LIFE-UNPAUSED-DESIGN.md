# Life, Unpaused: The Replacement Direction
October 14, 2026. This supersedes the previous Life, In Motion art direction after the owner rejected its claim of radical design. The project identity and preview address stay the same.

## Why The First Version Failed
It repeated the category's slogan/photo opening and linear brochure sequence. Serif typography, beige surfaces and an image switcher were styling changes, not the requested inversion. The sports-magazine suggestion was also insufficient on its own, because performance-led clinics already occupy that visual territory.

## Evidence To Decisions
See ANTI-TEMPLATE-RESEARCH.md for eight primary-source comparisons, six browser screenshot records, the refreshed bounded scrape and explicit counterexamples/limitations. The design contract is not “all clinics are blue.” It reverses the opening structure: clinic/conditions first becomes life-destination first; treatment-photo hero becomes an interactive typographic board; a stack of service cards becomes four oversized destination rows and a single contextual next-step area; brochure copy becomes an accessible reference library.

## Implemented Experience
- First screen: Life, Unpaused, with a four-stop destination board. No clinic-treatment or generic outdoor hero image.
- Choices: The Game, The Everyday, The Next Chapter and The Next Round. Large readable labels give equal status to athletic and everyday/postoperative goals. Smaller plain-language context distinguishes ACL injury, walking/balance, postoperative/new knee and golf/TPI, without age gates or protocols.
- Selecting a stop changes the destination word, context, route accent and actual care link. The hash records the choice for shareable/bookmarkable navigation, with no storage or health input. For example, `#everyday` reopens the everyday route.
- Each state offers a substantive care destination and a first-visit route. Insurance, what to bring and directions are immediately adjacent, not buried in a provider mega-menu.
- The signature graphic is a route trace, not patient motion or treatment footage. Brief destination/trace transitions occur only after a deliberate selection; reduced-motion preference disables animation/transitions.
- Source photography supports the real team and the care library below, rather than serving as a generic promise image.
- Full source content remains in all original page destinations and native homepage reference disclosures. The original logo, source media, noindex and integrated footer credit remain.

## Shared Visual System
Ink #142421, paper #F4F2EA, citrus #D6FA54, signal #FF855F. Active routes use additional pastel tints with explicit text/pressed states, so color is not the only indicator. Impact/Arial Narrow display roles replace Georgia; Arial body remains18px and long reference text remains plain, readable prose. This is deliberately an interface/poster composition rather than a sports-photo landing page. Display headings are content-sized, with a named long-word size variant for Everyday. No fixed-height filler, gated introduction, diagnosis quiz, fake progress score or autoplay.

Primary contrast ratios: paper/ink14.37:1, citrus/ink13.54:1, signal/ink6.73:1, clay/paper5.8:1. Keyboard focus remains visible. Older-adult readability is an acceptance condition, not an excuse to keep the rejected restrained styling.
