October 15, 2026. Execution: COM-518. Owner aesthetic disposition: COM-512, still open.

## The Critique

The lime version was a stronger graphic identity, but not a radical interaction. A giant campaign slogan, condensed sports type, four interchangeable tabs and an ornamental animated path are familiar fitness-brand devices. The decorative path communicated no progress or geography. Each click mostly exchanged a word, paragraph and link. The default athlete selection also gave sport priority over everyday mobility. Six portrait links all landed at the same team-page opening. These are concrete weaknesses; no real teenager was interviewed or claimed to approve this revision.

## Implemented Design

“Your Life Has Places To Be.” opens a material movement workshop. A worn court and shoe, a porch and steps, a hinged path through a doorway, and a putting green form distinct navigational objects. AI-generated miniature artwork is conceptual and identified in About This Preview; it is not clinic, patient or outcome evidence. Warm paper supports the physical objects; color and type are supporting changes, not the originality claim.

No destination is preselected. Each opens a guide with three meaningful routes, descriptions and a native disclosure containing a suggested question and office/first-visit actions. The guides concern editorial wayfinding, never diagnosis or a recovery protocol. Phone selection moves focus to the guide heading; Choose Another Destination returns to the chooser. Hashes remain bookmarkable. Keyboard operation, visible focus and reduced-motion support remain. Without JavaScript, direct fallback links and the overview's care/visit/class links remain available.

All six portrait links now land on the corresponding biography. Full source-copy dispositions, existing routes, noindex disclosure, inactive collection, and integrated footer credit are retained. The original logo remains the source transparent PNG.
