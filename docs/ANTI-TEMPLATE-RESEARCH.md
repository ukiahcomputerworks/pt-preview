## Explicit Inversion Contract
| Repeating Convention | Opposite Decision In This Revision | Observable Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clinic introduces itself with a recovery slogan over a large treatment/lifestyle hero photo | Homepage opens as an activity navigator and typographic poster, Life, Unpaused; no treatment-photo hero | First screen is the visitor's goal interface, not a stock treatment scene plus appointment button |
| Services/body areas organize the first decision | Life destinations organize the entry: sport, daily independence, postoperative life, golf | Each selection provides a practical next route and visit/coverage information; no diagnostic body map |
| Generic personalized-care claims carry the emotional promise | Short, concrete goals and transparent navigation carry the first interaction | Existing clinical copy remains in expandable reference content, not a rewritten miracle claim |
| Repeated boxed-card grids and a long brochure scroll | Oversized numbered typographic route rows, one changing destination panel, compact care disclosures | Main choices are navigation controls, not promotional cards; full source content remains accessible |
| Calm medical/blue-white or tasteful wellness styling | Ink, signal orange, citrus and oversized graphic typography; original gold/black logo stays intact | Screenshot-level difference in composition, typography and interaction, beyond a palette swap |
| Sports-performance branding defaults to young elite athletes | Daily independence gets equal graphic weight and its own route | No age gate; new knee and ACL examples are contextual, not age assumptions |
| Decorative autoplay/video drives engagement | Visitor-driven goal changes; brief optional spatial motion, no required animation or intro gate | Keyboard and reduced-motion paths expose the same information immediately |

## New Direction: Life, Unpaused
An interactive destination board rather than a clinic brochure. The big first decision is where life should go next. Goal selection changes the destination word, route marker, short context and actual next-step links. A graphic route diagram is the signature, with documentary source photography used as supporting evidence lower in the selected journey, not as a generic opening hero. The full source library, actual people, coverage and contact remain one action away. No game score, treatment prescription, fake recovery timeline, fabricated testimonial or forced slang.

## Acceptance Limit
This is an implementation of an explicit inversion brief, not proof that a real teenager approves it. Owner review remains open. The source-content, clinical/privacy, logo, noindex, compact-layout and production boundaries remain in force.
