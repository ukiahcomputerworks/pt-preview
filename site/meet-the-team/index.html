Meet The Team

01 / Your Care Team

William Binns

Owner, PT, DPT, CF- L1, Titleist Performance Institute Certified

Dr Binns, a resident of Ukiah, has a passion for helping people reach their highest potential with a hands-on approach. He graduated from Sonoma State University with a BS in Kinesiology and earned his Doctor of Physical Therapy degree in 2017 from The University of St. Augustine in Austin, Texas where he learned specialized manual therapy treatment techniques developed by Stanley Paris. These skills enable him to help his patients achieve great results by identifying the root source of their problem and developing a treatment plan to address and correct the identified issues.

Read The Full Biography +

Billy specializes in orthopedic manual therapy, sports rehabilitation and chronic pain by using his knowledge of current research and evidence-based practice. He has extensive training with olympic lifting and specializes in the rehabilitation of Crossfit and endurance athletes. He is a Crossfit Level 1 trainer. He is certified by the Titleist Performance Institute to perform the golf-specific TPI assessments which help golfers improve their swing mechanics, build power and reduce injuries.

02 / Your Care Team

Victor Hernandez

PT, DPT

Dr Hernandez is a lifelong resident of Ukiah. Born and raised in the community, he grew up participating in various sports, with soccer being his primary focus. He attended Ukiah High School before continuing his education at Mendocino College, where he also competed as a member of the soccer team. His athletic dedication earned him a soccer scholarship to the University of Saint Mary in Leavenworth, Kansas. After a year in Kansas, he returned to California and completed his bachelor’s degree at Sonoma State University in 2019.

Read The Full Biography +

Following graduation, Victor returned to Mendocino College as part of the inaugural cohort of the Physical Therapist Assistant (PTA) program, earning his PTA degree in 2021. He went on to work locally as a PTA, where he recognized the growing need for additional providers in the field. Motivated to meet that need, he pursued advanced training and completed his Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT) degree at Hawai‘i Pacific University in 2024. His combined experience as both a PTA and a licensed physical therapist allows him to deliver comprehensive, patient-centered care.

Outside of his professional work, Victor enjoys spending time with his wife, their young daughter, and the family’s pet turtle. He is an outdoor enthusiast who often hikes and participates in a variety of outdoor activities. A family-oriented individual, he remains closely connected with his three brothers and his parents.

Victor is also deeply involved in the local soccer community. As a coach with the Mendocino County Soccer Academy (MCSA), he serves as one of the program’s goalkeeper coaches, helping young athletes develop their skills while fostering a positive and enjoyable environment.

Fluent in Spanish and experienced in athletics, Victor is well-equipped to work with athletes and individuals recovering from orthopedic injuries. He finds great fulfillment in helping patients manage pain, recover from injury, and improve their overall well-being. Victor is committed to one-on-one care and takes a holistic approach to treatment, ensuring each patient receives personalized support tailored to their goals.

03 / Your Care Team

Pauline Bowser

PT, DPT

Pauline's path to physical therapy has been anything but traditional. Originally from Sacramento, she's explored a wide range of careers and experiences - from living in France and studying the language and culture, researching plague ecology in prairie dogs in the Badlands of South Dakota, working as a veterinary technician, to indulging her love of baking along the way. These varied experiences have given her a deep appreciation for the many different ways people live, work, and move through the world, and for the many demands life can place on the body.

Read The Full Biography +

Pauline earned her Doctor of Physical Therapy from Eastern Washington University in May 2026 and chose to bring her skills back to the Mendocino community. She has a special interest in neurologic rehabilitation and enjoys working with a broad range of individuals, meeting each person where they are and building practical, individualized care around the goals and activities that matter most to them. During PT school, she also had the opportunity to share her love of rock climbing with people of all abilities through an adaptive climbing program, an experience she found especially rewarding.

Pauline's approach to physical therapy centers on education - understanding the "why" behind movement and helping people feel confident in their own bodies. She believes that truly understanding your condition and treatment plan empowers you to take an active role in your recovery. She views herself as a partner and cheerleader throughout the rehabilitation process, working alongside each patient to help them feel strong, capable, and confident at every stage of life.

Outside the clinic, you'll find Pauline soaking up the outdoors, weightlifting, or spending time with her husband and their four fur babies. She looks forward to getting to know you and being part of your journey toward moving, living, and feeling your best.

In 2021, she graduated from Mendocino College with her PTA degree. Over the last 30 years, she has also worked as an aide in several local PT offices.

Married for 30 years, Jani and her husband have raised two boys and now have a grandchild whom they enjoy spoiling. Her primary hobbies include exercise, nutrition, and reading. Mykala Ramsey, PTA

Read The Full Biography +

Mykala Ramsey is a native of Redwood Valley with a lifelong passion for sports, movement, and maintaining an active lifestyle. Growing up, she participated in a variety of sports, playing soccer and softball throughout high school and continuing her athletic career as a softball player at Mendocino College. This experience inspired her to pursue a career helping others achieve their rehabilitation goals.

Outside of the clinic, Mykala enjoys spending time with her family, exploring the surrounding mountains in her Jeep with friends, staying active, and volunteering in her church’s youth and children’s programs.
