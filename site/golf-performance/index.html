Golf Performance

Titleist Performance Institute Certified

We offer specialized assessments tailored to golfers, aiming to enhance swing mechanics, increase power, and minimize the risk of injuries. These assessments are designed to evaluate key aspects of a golfer's physical fitness, such as flexibility, strength, and mobility, which directly impact their performance on the course. By identifying areas for improvement and implementing targeted exercises and training programs, TPI helps golfers optimize their game and achieve their full potential.

What Is A TPI Assessment?

Body Screen:

PT evaluation and 16 step screen designed to help identify your body’s limitations and any source of pain

Swing Analysis:

Help identify any swing faults that may be affecting shot consistency or resulting in pain.

Training Session:

Personalized corrective exercise program designed to address deficits found in your body screen and swing analysis
