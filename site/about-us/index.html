About Us

At Mendocino Physical Therapy, we specialize in utilizing cutting-edge orthopedic manual therapy techniques and tailored exercise programs to enhance mobility and promote pain-free movement. With a hands-on approach, our dedicated team aims to address the root cause of each patient's condition, ensuring comprehensive and effective treatment. Our therapists are extensively trained in advanced manual therapy techniques, post-operative care, and geriatric rehabilitation.

Our Facilities

Equipped with a wide range of tools, from barbells to resistance bands, our facilities are designed to support diverse rehabilitation needs, whether you're a professional athlete or recovering from surgery. Our inviting atmosphere ensures that clients feel comfortable and supported throughout their treatment journey. With two convenient locations, we are committed to providing accessible and exceptional care to our community.
