Insurance

We accept most Insurance and Worke’s Compensation cases. We unfortunately are not Medi-Cal providers and do not accept Auto Accident Liens but will bill your personal auto insurance if you have available MedPay. We always as a courtesy bill your insurance for you regardless if we are contracted with your insurance or not.
