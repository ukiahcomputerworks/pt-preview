About This Preview

A new direction for Mendocino Physical Therapy, created for design review.

This is an independent website preview, not the practice’s production website. Practice content, photographs and logo have been brought over from its public website for this review. Schedules, benefits and provider information should be confirmed with the office.

The destination guides provide editorial links only. The miniature objects are AI-generated conceptual illustrations, not photographs of the practice, patients or outcomes. The preview does not accept messages, completed patient forms or health information. Class reservations open the practice’s existing external scheduling service. External services and hosting may process information under their own policies.
